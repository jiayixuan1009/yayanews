Duplicate URL:

```text
https://yayanews.cryptooptiontool.com/zh/article/jin-jie-xin-gao-bei-hou-qi-quan-yin-han-bo-dong-lu-biao-sheng-yu-jing-shi-chang
```

This is not the earlier multilingual hreflang question. It is a true exact duplicate `<loc>` caused by article sitemap pagination. Code review shows `getRecentArticlesForSitemap()` orders only by `a.published_at DESC`, then paginates with `LIMIT/OFFSET`. When many rows share identical timestamps, adjacent chunks can overlap.

Impact:

- Low direct SEO impact because it is a single duplicate among 6,641 URLs.
- Still worth fixing because sitemap chunks should be deterministic and exact-duplicate-free.

Recommended fix:

- Add a stable secondary sort to article sitemap queries, for example:

```sql
ORDER BY COALESCE(a.published_at, a.created_at) DESC, a.id DESC
```

Acceptance criteria:

- Main sitemap exact duplicate URL count becomes `0`.
- Article chunk boundaries remain stable across repeated requests.
- Do not collapse zh/en translation groups. Keep localized URLs as separate `<loc>` entries with alternates.

## Content And CTR Diagnosis

GSC Performance shows impressions but almost no clicks.

The highest-impression pages are article pages with long pinyin slugs and long analytical Chinese titles. Queries are also long-tail finance/investment queries.

Likely causes of low CTR:

1. Search result titles may read generic or overly similar across many articles.
2. Article titles often use the same structure: market move + cause + analysis.
3. Snippets may not clearly signal why the result is better than competing sources.
4. Brand trust is still weak, so even ranking positions can generate low click-through.
5. Some high-impression pages may be surfacing for broad informational queries where the title does not match the exact intent.

Recommended workflow:

1. Export GSC Performance Pages + Queries.
2. For top 20 high-impression/zero-click pages, map each page to its top queries.
3. Rewrite title/H1/description for query match and click value.
4. Add visible analyst/reviewer/source/date trust blocks to those pages.
5. Recheck CTR after 7-14 days.
